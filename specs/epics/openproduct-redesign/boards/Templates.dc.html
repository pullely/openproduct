Five kinds of post, and nothing else

openproduct is not a place to share anything. Every post is one of these templates, filled in field by field, so posts of the same kind look the same and can be compared, filtered and trusted.

Every post shows who and whatThe product it's about and the person who posted it. Both can be followed.
Automatic posts say soDemos and deployment status come from Orun and say so. A demo names the maker who approved it.
Talk lives under postsComments, replies and reactions are open on every post. There is no free-standing status update.
Only makers post for a productYou can post only for products you're on the crew of.
